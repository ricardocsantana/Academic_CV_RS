\section{Summary}
\small{Ph.D. researcher at KTH working on \textbf{conversational and multimodal AI for human--robot collaboration}. I build robots that decide \emph{where} to look, \emph{when} to speak and \emph{whether} to act, by grounding full-duplex spoken language models in a shared task and steering them with predicted partner state, from self-reported enjoyment to brain activity. Background in machine learning and deep reinforcement learning (TU Delft) and GenAI strategy (McKinsey Digital). \textbf{ICMI 2025 Best Paper Award}.}
